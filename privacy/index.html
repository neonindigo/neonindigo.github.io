Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who We Are

NeonIndigo is a trading name of Michael Robinson. We develop apps for Apple platforms. If you have any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@neonindigo.com.

2. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, subscribe to product news, use a NeonIndigo app, or contact us for support. It does not apply to third-party services or websites we do not operate.

3. Information We Collect

Website analytics. We use Umami to understand aggregate website traffic and actions such as visits to an app page or clicks through to the App Store. Our website analytics are not connected to newsletter email addresses, and we do not use them for advertising.

Product news subscriptions. If you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address, consent status, signup date, and the app or website form through which you subscribed. We use Kit to manage subscriptions, confirmation emails, product-news emails, and unsubscribes. Kit requires new subscribers to confirm their address before joining the list.

Pseudonymous app analytics. Some of our apps use Amplitude to collect analytics data that helps us understand how the apps are used and improve them. This data includes:

  • Product interactions, such as app launches, screens viewed, and features used
  • A device-level identifier assigned or used by the analytics service
  • Device category (e.g. iPhone, iPad, Mac)
  • App version and operating system version
  • Approximate country or region derived from the network connection

This analytics data is linked to a device-level identifier, but not to your name, email address, or Apple ID. We do not use it to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, or for advertising.

Server and infrastructure logs. Our analytics infrastructure may automatically generate logs that include IP addresses, request timestamps, and similar technical data. These logs are used solely for security and operational purposes.

Support correspondence. If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any personal information you include in your message.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

  • Website analytics: Legitimate interests — understanding website use and whether our product information is useful.
  • Product news subscriptions: Consent — sending the major app launches and meaningful product updates you asked to receive.
  • Pseudonymous app analytics: Legitimate interests — understanding use of our apps, improving features, and prioritising support for countries and regions.
  • Server logs (including IP addresses): Legitimate interests — ensuring the security and operational integrity of our systems.
  • Support emails: Legitimate interests — providing support to users who contact us.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To improve our apps and services
  • To understand how visitors use our website
  • To send product news to people who have confirmed their subscription
  • To understand usage by country or region and prioritise localisation
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To maintain the security of our systems

6. Data Retention

  • Website analytics: Retained for up to 12 months, after which it is deleted or irreversibly aggregated.
  • Product news subscriptions: Retained while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we may retain the minimum information necessary to honour that request and demonstrate consent.
  • Pseudonymous app analytics: Retained for up to 12 months, after which it is deleted or irreversibly aggregated.
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 30 days.
  • Support correspondence: Retained for as long as necessary to resolve your query, and no longer than 2 years thereafter.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We use Umami for website analytics, Kit for email subscriptions and product-news delivery, and Amplitude for analytics in some apps. These providers process the relevant data described above on our behalf. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the provider’s contractual and organisational safeguards. We may also disclose personal data where required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

8. Your Rights

Where we process personal data about you (for example, via a product-news subscription, pseudonymous analytics, server logs, or support emails), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request rectification or erasure of your data
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent to product-news emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@neonindigo.com. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of our apps after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@neonindigo.com.
